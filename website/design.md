# The work of becoming.

Singularity Clicker follows human development through technological and social changes. Each stage introduces a different way to play while carrying forward the theme of accumulated knowledge.

## Stage 1: The Hearth

The first Hearth journey targets roughly two hours of meaningful progress. That is a design target still awaiting playtesting, not a countdown.

### Your people

Select villagers and assign tasks by right-clicking or dragging them onto work. Touch controls provide selection followed by tapping a target. Readable clothing and tools show the assignment.

A woodcutter gathers, carries, deposits, and repeats. A farmer performs the available field work. Builders turn your outlines into structures. Villagers rest when work is blocked and resume when conditions allow.

No enemies, combat, starvation, or villager deaths. Hunting is a separate food-gathering activity, not hostile combat.

### Your hearth

The fire automatically uses a small amount of wood from the nearby stockpile. An empty pile gives time for another delivery. If supplies remain exhausted, the elders call for migration. The community carries its ember and knowledge onward.

### Your landscape

Each overworld hex opens a local landscape with a central hearth site. Place structures, storage, fields, fences, and gates. Return to find earlier improvements and the consequences of harvesting.

Nearby hexes are visible. High elevation extends sight to two hexes unless intervening high ground blocks the view.

Selective harvesting leaves future growth. Clearing field stones yields both material and useful ground. More developed transport makes provisioned gathering and quarry routes productive. The hidden discoveries are left for players to find.

### Your progression

Practical skills lead into better tools, bindings, storage, preservation, pottery, cultivation, animal management, and draft power. The proposed endpoint is the first working animal-drawn plow, supported by a functioning community.

Cultivation starts before that endpoint. Stage 2 makes agriculture the organizing center of life and expands its scale. Historical chronology is regional and compressed; the game should distinguish historical evidence from invented discovery episodes.

### Your next journey

Optional prestige after completion preserves the finished world and starts a fresh journey with inherited traditions. Ordinary migration is not a reset. Returning players also benefit from their own knowledge of resources and geography.

## Platforms

The browser comes first. Every stage should also be designed for eventual macOS and iOS release. Simulation, rendering, input, and saved data should remain separable so native packaging does not require rewriting game rules.

The existing prototypes use directly authored SVG and Canvas artwork. No image-generator assets.

## Development status

The Cave and a first playable Hearth prototype have been built. Both browser stages are included in this repository and built by the Pages workflow. Native binaries, human/device playtesting, and later stages are not complete.
