WORK AT YOUR OWN PACE

Give an order.
Watch a home take shape.

The Hearth is cozy through useful work: a woodpile filling, a fence taking shape, and people returning to the fire. There are no enemies, villager deaths, or frantic survival timers.

Automation grows out of skills and good organization. Clever choices can make a faster journey; taking your time remains part of the pleasure.

A LONGER HORIZON

From tools to machines to the singularity.

Later ages bring new forms of play, from farming to industrial production and computing. Fourteen stages are envisioned, ending at Stage 13: the singularity. Later stages and native editions remain in design.
