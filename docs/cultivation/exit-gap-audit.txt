Conclusion
----------
Recommend a bounded local H2 milestone, “Equip a working camp”: finite fiber,
physically collected bounded water, explicitly activated prospective support,
per-worker baskets and ordinary/sharp tool choices, plus the declared pilot-crop
recovery path. The player should make a real carrying-versus-extraction investment
and see its effect on the same finite route, while supporting that work through
ordinary gathering/cultivation. This advances beyond opening feedback without
pretending that First Furrow or Agriculture handoff is available.

The first implementation decision is profile compatibility, not new gameplay:
v6's earned global Tools018 benefit is not EM01's per-worker tool economy. Preserve
that exact playable profile. A new EM01 local path must be explicitly identified
as a new/adopted profile with an independently reviewed mapping, or an identified
fresh full-age path with the declared seed. Do not silently replace a paid .45
toolset with .48 ordinary tools, erase paid work, or grant four new per-worker
tools from a generic completed-tools Boolean. The bounded milestone can leave
cross-world authority and full-age completion unavailable.

Two bounded playable candidates
-------------------------------
A — Equip a working camp (recommended richer-Hearth step).
  Freeze compatibility/content row first. Preserve accepted opening and earned
  exact old profile. In the new explicitly identified EM01 path, gather finite
  fiber/real water, quote and activate prospective support, craft/equip a basket
  and ordinary tool on named workers, learn one funded sharp-head plan, and use
  the same long haul to choose extraction investment versus cargo investment.
  Include pilot crop and the protected next-cycle seed path so support has a
  visible affordable recovery route. K.treeplot becomes available after pilot
  under its exact contract. This is a substantial local economic loop, still
  only a partial H2/Hearth implementation. Do not promote unused rack/cart/plow
  placeholders or unlock Agriculture. Full root/cross-world writes stay gated.

B — A tended supply patch (smaller, closer-to-Agriculture alternative).
  Same explicit profile/support/source boundary, but prioritize discovered seed,
  one actual pilot crop, repeat seed custody and competing funded tree plot.
  Retain finite food gathering as a viable alternative; water collection and
  named worker allocation make farming a decision rather than free growth.
  Observe a complete planting→unattended foreground growth→harvest→second funded
  planting cycle and one fuel-plot decision. Defer new tool recipes, animal
  training, regional travel and plow. This advances cultivation before the age
  capstone without claiming Agriculture or giving EM02's later grant early.
  It has less transport depth than A; avoid calling it complete H2.

Why not choose immediate regional travel or Agriculture now? Both are meaningful
but require missing shared ownership, root persistence and safe return contracts.
They are defensible later milestones, not a link or a cloned old-state shortcut.
Why not another throughput panel? v6 already supplies the observation; the next
milestone must change what the workers can physically do and what the player funds.
