CHOICE COMPARISON
A — Provision and equip: fiber/water/support, basket, shelter/new invitations.
Useful immediate choice: improve one carrier or buy housing/labor. A basket has
an observable8-versus5 load, while a recruit is a new named actor with an actual
job. This can be a coherent milestone and the architecture review's choice is
reasonable. It is not the smallest complete support/recovery loop, however:
new recruitment spends finite food and adds berth/roster/invitation custody while
crop/fuel renewal stays missing. Shipping support before any cultivated recovery
would require especially honest finite limits and preserved recovery funding.
Do not claim recruitment is required for the canonical completion; EM01 says it
is optional. Shelter without a functioning invitation is an unconsumed recipe.

B — Provision, carry, grow: fiber/water/support, basket, pilot crop and tree plot.
Stronger than A for recurring support: hauling investment helps collect supplies,
and cultivation creates a funded alternative to depleting wild patches. It also
has the largest implementation surface of these choices: every five-item cargo
boundary, mixed-capacity reservations, equipment ownership and observer samples
must change. Basket is an actual benefit, but it is separable from cultivation.

B0 — A tended supply patch: water/seed/support, pilot crop and tree plot only.
Smallest coherent scope because it keeps existing cargo5, selection, delivery,
construction and named workers while adding a real production lifecycle. Its
decision is immediate wild gathering versus planting a slower repeatable crop,
then food replenishment versus a fuel plot using real water/seed/land/labor.
It should be presented as local cultivation, not a completed H2 "better trip"
or the ordinary/basket/sharp tool comparison. No universal efficiency advantage
is promised: abundant nearby wild food/wood can rationally be the better choice.

MINIMUM COHERENT B0 PRODUCT RESULT
1. At the unchanged completed opening, the player can inspect the next opportunity
   and the exact prospective support rules before activating them. Inspection
   spends nothing or alters no orders. Retaining the exact opening profile is
   a real option, with its existing recruits and Tools018 behavior intact.
2. The player discovers the authored six-seed cache and physical water source,
   uses existing workers to collect/haul, then funds a selected pilot crop.
   Inputs, occupied patch, assigned worker and present phase are visible.
3. Crop maturation continues only during active foreground simulation, without
   an artificial assigned worker or support debit for unattended growth. Workers
   can do useful gathering/building/hauling while the crop grows.
4. Mature output is real food and retained seed at a named location. It is
   collected/delivered once. The player can see those goods fund real support
   or another useful action, rather than merely increment a completion counter.
5. The first completed pilot makes the canonical tree plot usable in this local
   preview. The exact unlock event must be frozen; an actual pilot harvest is
   the clearest recommended event, not opening a panel. The tree's real wood
   and retained seed are harvested/delivered and can fund fuel or construction.
6. Two crop deliveries plus one actual tree harvest may define this local
   milestone, provided they are useful delivered goods, not ceremonial counts.
   A quoted next cycle remains funded. This record never aliases opening.complete
   or First Furrow. The player may continue/stop freely after earning it.

TWO FEASIBLE ORDERS, NOT TWO COSMETIC BUTTONS
Demonstrate from the same authentic earned opening:
- Plant food early, then perform useful wild gathering or a real local delivery
  while it grows; use the first crop's output, run the second crop, then tree.
- Gather wild food first to support immediate useful work; plant the pilot, then
  invest in the unlocked tree before the second food crop. While wood matures,
  workers perform remaining funded useful tasks, and food/seed funding remains
  safe. If the authored patch geometry permits parallel beds, show their actual
  distinct occupied land rather than overlapping output on one tile.
Both orders ultimately meet the same two-crop/one-tree milestone. They should
differ in support buffer, worker occupation, delivery timing and finite source
use. Do not claim a land competition if the final layout gives each recipe an
independent unconstrained site; describe the real water/seed/labor trade instead.

The tree's300 growth-seconds are canonical but are a pacing risk in a small
preview. It is acceptable as an unattended production choice with useful work
alongside it. It is not acceptable to rush every other task then force a solved
five-minute idle tail and call that substantive progression. Do not shorten or
pad the canonical timer silently; choose a route/sequence with actual concurrent
work and record any remaining wait honestly. Human pacing still needs a human
verdict, distinct from deterministic progression.

The proposal selects B0, preserves v6 explicitly, names a bounded local tree-site
mapping, defers new recruitment with disclosure, preserves physically carried
seed/output, and protects a funded next food cycle. Those choices resolve the
major product-scope questions. Two crop deliveries and one woodland output used
are appropriate acceptance witness outcomes. The text explicitly says they are
not a new arbitrary unlock timer/age-exit gate: retain that distinction. Do not
later wire the witness count to recipe access, compulsory ending, handoff or a
forced celebration. Woodland's own canonical earned unlock remains separate.

Three concrete gameplay clarifications remain before freezing:
G1. The opening paragraph claims food/wood renewal compete for land, but the
    declared content has one fixed pilot plot and one separate fixed woodland
    plot. That is not yet a land-allocation choice. Remove "land" from the
    competition claim unless a genuine shared occupancy constraint is specified.
    Labor, water and seed already provide sufficient meaningful competition;
    no additional placement system is needed for this milestone.
G2. Bind "after the first pilot crop" to one exact earned state/event. Recommend
    the first real completed pilot output receipt/harvest, identified by its job,
    not discovery, a reservation, button click, or opening a panel. Pick the
    exact event once and give the UI/validator/test the same definition.
G3. State how a player assigns planting, releases workers for unattended growth,
    resumes interrupted paid work, and collects both resource types at maturity.
    With current single-resource cargo5, six food/two seed or eight wood/two seed
    needs multiple physical trips. A food-only collection action must not leave
    retained seed invisibly blocking the next batch. The tray should explain
    remaining items and the ordinary next collection action, while no offscreen
    auto-assignment cancels another useful worker order. This needs an observable
    interaction contract, not a new output-grant shortcut.

The marked pending numeric quantum, pantry return, support allocation and source
coordinates are real freeze blockers and remain with technical/custody review.
Their presence is honest; this gameplay report does not choose them by accident
or declare them implemented. Subject to G1–G3 and those technical decisions,
the selected B0 is a coherent, bounded next playable progression milestone.
