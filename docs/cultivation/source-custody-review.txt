Arithmetic and certificate reasoning

ledger.js:14-31 keeps support in exact third-milli arithmetic. Effective owned
pantry is3*milli-remainder; posted consumed and remainder are tied to aggregate
demand/camp slots. Each demanding worker slot accrues1third-milli food/water;
the first demanding slot per camp tick accrues5third-milli wood, controlled by
engine._fuelPaid. Subsequent simultaneous actors do not debit camp fuel again.
No posted-debit-zero slot is free: admission compares the rational prospective
debit, not only its rounded posted milliunits. Whole pantry top-ups move1000
milli per actual b0 item and do not increment legacy spent. Whole returns keep
fractional residual/liability owned. The old paid capital equality stays separate.

balance subtracts actual source reservations, roster floor, all ACTIVE remaining
slot bounds, NEXT support and NEXT's separate three whole recipe water. Generic
available() limits optional whole spending to whole amounts above that balance.
The raw reservation functions retain material in source stock until actual
pickup; they do not count a reservation as extra inventory.

A named job's allocation releases only its own one-slot bound while checking
the debit. With multiple workers, each successful work slot decreases remaining
work; fuel is paid once and redundant conservative allowance becomes free only
as justified by completed work. Bare collection, walking, receipt, growth and
blocked work do not use allocateSupport. Tick resets the one-camp fuel flag;
legacy completion stops later helpers from receiving work on the completed job.

jobs.js:13-17 quotes normal ACTIVE+NEXT using a cloned candidate. If normal pilot
funding fails but the existing NEXT can fund the same pilot, it quotes the
explicit protected recovery action with nextFunded=false. The real admission
installs those sources/job once; it does not require a replacement NEXT first.
This implements the reviewed one-paid-continuation guarantee. refreshNext can
restore NEXT only when local exact funding exists; it never fabricates water
or food. Tree admission requires funded NEXT and its separate recipe/support.
New construction and new Tools projects are refused while NEXT is unfunded;
the corrected extraction path also respects that discretionary block.

The current top-up loop does not explicitly subtract NEXT's three whole water
before transfer, but its prior full rational balance/floor check leaves enough
physical source water in the reviewed reachable cases. No definite siphoning
case was found. QA was asked to assert independently on every witnessed step:
when nextFunded, b0 water minus transfer, physical-build and cultivation input
source reservations is at least three. This is a useful boundary witness, not
a second claimed defect.

Material jobs and exact-once boundaries

jobs.js:25/:35-46 follows reserve -> physical pickup -> staged -> first paid
work -> growing -> harvest/output -> physical receipt. Source lines remain in
their actual stores until pickup. Carried input/recovery/output has a job tag
and contributes once to the global material equation. First planting consumes
recipe water once and moves the two staged seed into seedLot. At output it
moves that same quantity to the output tray, not newly generated seed.
Generated food/wood are recorded once at completion and reconciled with batch
history. Output pickup is seed-first, one resource and at most5 per load.
Output remains owned at the plot or by its carrier until a real store receipt.
The woodland unlock follows a tagged pilot-food receipt, not maturation or seed.

Stop and loaded Move retain tagged custody. Returning an interrupted input
restores the source reservation at the actual receiving store. Cancel before
paid planting releases unpicked source reservations and physically returns
staged/carried inputs; started work is resumable and is not refunded into both
supplies and a future output. Cancel repetition is inert. Repeated plant on an
existing batch returns its identity without another reservation/bill. Complete
plot removal waits until output/recovery is no longer staged/carried.

ledger.js:47-52 counts legacy stock/spent/cargo/deposits/construction stage,
new stage/output/seedLot, pantry plus posted support consumption, recipe-water
consumption and the actual water pool once. The RHS is original finite totals,
declared admitted water flow and typed food/wood outputs. Old payment/reclaim
equalities in engine validBase/canonicalReason/validReclaim remain exact; no
v6 validation projection disguises fractional consumption as free stock.
