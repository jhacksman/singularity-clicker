Whole items plus exact support pantry

Whole-item lanes remain bounded nonnegative safe integers: store piles, cargo,
finite wood/stone/food/fiber amounts, recipe bills, legacy spent and source
reservation lines. All legacy resource fields retain their value and unit.
Add water whole items in stores/cargo; its source pool is separately milliunit
storage. Physical pickup removes exactly 1000 pool milliunits per one water
item and requires that whole amount. Less than one item remains at the source;
it neither rounds up to cargo nor vanishes. Draw/pause/wait behavior must be
explicit and must not busy-loop free successful extractions at an empty pool.

Support state names the supplying store/camp and holds, per resource:
pantryMilli, postedConsumedMilli, chargeRemainder, and the pinned denominator.
Keep immutable opening/adoption identity plus bounded cumulative transfers
and consumption, not an unbounded per-tick event log. Use checked BigInt
arithmetic for the new ledger and decimal strings on its wire fields; validate
legacy integers before converting them. Bounds apply before multiplication
or addition. No Number float multiplication by a support rate, overflow clamp,
epsilon balance check, or independent debit/credit rounding is acceptable.

A transfer of one whole food from store b1 credits exactly 1000 food milliunits
to b1's named pantry. It consumes nothing and changes no legacy spent. The
unit is removed from the store, so builders, transfers and invitations cannot
spend it again. A remote pantry is not spendable camp-wide cash. If multiple
stores supply support, each has its own custody/obligation attribution and
actual supported locality; never silently move fractional material between
them. Starting with one declared support store is the smaller implementation.

At one 100-ms demanding worker tick, food and water each accrue 1/3 milliunit.
At one active demanding camp tick, wood accrues 5/3 milliunits ONCE for that
camp, regardless of its number of workers. For each account compute:
  numerator = old remainder + exact rate numerator * admitted activity
  posted debit = numerator div denominator
  new remainder = numerator mod denominator
Debit postedMilli and credit postedConsumedMilli once. Save the remainder.
For integer-millisecond partial allocations the equivalent denominators are
300 for food/water and 60 for fuel, with activity in milliseconds. Aggregate
camp fuel uses the UNION of active intervals, not summed worker milliseconds.
Do not mix these two representations without a certified remainder mapping.

The remainder is an accrued fractional liability. Effective pantry material
is pantryMilli - remainder/denominator, and exact consumed support is
postedConsumedMilli + remainder/denominator. This pair preserves the exact
rational total. The posted integer conservation equation separately remains
exact. Floors and next-quantum admission must include that liability, even
when the next posted integer debit is zero. Otherwise two ticks at a floor
can perform free demanding work before the third tick records a milliunit.
Pause, reassign, reload, cancellation, profile views and support shortages
must never erase, redistribute or multiply the remainder.

Unspent pantry is still owned material. Unprovision/cancel may return only
funded whole units to that same store; a residual fraction and its accrued
liability remain explicitly owned and inspectable in the pantry. Never round
999 milliunits to either zero or a free whole item. No cross-store refund or
outpost reclaim may erase its pantry: recover/refund reachable whole amounts
and retain/move the actual residual by an explicitly implemented custody
operation, or refuse retiring that store with a precise explanation.

The profile must quote when pantry transfer happens and how it is replenished.
An explicit standing authorization to top up from eligible local free stock
avoids requiring a manual refill each time; it creates no stock. A transfer
batch must not impose an undisclosed extra whole-unit tax or unnecessarily
block a feasible quantum. Protection/availability is calculated across local
free whole stock plus effective pantry, minus obligations, so merely splitting
one unit does not change affordability. Recipe payment still needs whole
physical inputs; pantry fractions are not spare construction units.
