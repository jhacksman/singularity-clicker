1. Supported physical recovery row
Profile row: accepted-opening.outpost-reclaim.v1.
Recover a certified, completed, empty and settled optional outpost. The hearth
and workshop are excluded. Its structural vector is exactly 60 wood + 20 stone,
returned once at 100%. Stored goods, other cargo and spent work are separate.
Do not refund construction labor, recruit food, paid tool inputs or consumables.

This is a finite 'Recover this outpost until finished or interrupted' assignment,
not the existing store-transfer button's single wave. Explicitly selected
existing villagers perform real adjacent pickup, path travel and receipt using
the accepted cadence and movement rate of 3 cells/second. Each carries up to
five units of one resource. There is no new speed, support cost or separately
timed demolition phase. Duration is the actual handling/travel of those workers;
it is not a fixed completion-time promise or an authored zero-second demolition
recipe. This is the minimal physical-job interpretation reviewed against the
specification, which does not require a second timed stage.

At each ordinary pickup transition, revalidate current assignment, empty hands,
exact claim/site identity, real adjacency, a usable receiving route, and spent
backing. Choose wood before stone and fill min(5, that resource's unreserved
remaining amount); update each actor atomically in stable actor-ID order. Do not
split otherwise full tranches across workers. Starting from 60W/20S this requires
twelve wood loads and four stone loads. Reservations do not release materials.
Pickup never occurs in quote or command handlers; repeat input cannot add another
pickup/receipt transition within the actor's ordinary simulation boundary.
Legal shared pickup/receipt edges may have zero-length travel. Keep separate
physical pickup and receipt transitions; invent no minimum walk or extra delay.

For the picked-up vector L, atomically:
  spent -= L; claim.unreleased -= L; named actor's tagged cargo += L.
There is no instantaneous whole-structure release and no separate site pile.
At an actual eligible store receipt:
  tagged cargo -= L; reached store += L; claim.receipted += L.
Only received stock is spendable. Reclaimed cargo does not increment gathered,
transferred-from-a-store or depleted counters. Show recovered receipts separately.

Per claim: 60W20S = unreleased + its tagged cargo + cumulative receipted.
Per certified world: T = remaining wild + real stores + ALL actor cargo + spent.
Receipted and other cumulative metrics are attribution, not extra inventory.
The remaining paid material includes unreleased capital and all other paid
obligations; do not add claims/reservations a second time. New construction paid
with received salvage creates its own claim; it never reinstates the old one.

2. Admission, service and input ownership
Quote is read-only. Commit validates the complete selected group before changing
any worker order, allocation or site service: existing stable IDs, empty hands,
no incompatible live obligation and a path through one actual pickup edge to an
eligible non-retiring receiver. Two unrelated reachable edges do not prove that
route. A rejected group/order preserves the existing serialized economic state.
Only after validation may a valid replacement release an actor's old assignment.

The outpost must have zero actual stock and no unsettled incoming, unpicked,
queued, transfer or refundable-source obligation. Inspect durable transfer
markers/job endpoints even after manual Move removed dropoff. Historical
completed-payment references alone do not block recovery. Explain the exact
live blocker and its ordinary settlement action.

At successful commitment, mark the site retiring and exclude it from every new
home/dropoff, funding/pay/nearestStore, transfer/receipt and queued admission.
Revalidate at commitment and share this eligibility rule across all entry paths.
A store that was empty at quote time must not gain new promises during recovery.
The original 2x2 footprint stays occupied until settlement; its finite unreleased
claim accepts no unrelated deposits and is not a general storage/cache feature.

Keep every unsettled recovery site in placement/topology protection even after
it stops being isStore(). Construction must not seal its last usable pickup edge.
Check actual pickup-edge-to-receiver continuity again before pickup. Retiring
another receiving store must account for these dependencies or prove a usable
alternative. If a route disappears, preserve unreleased capital or held cargo
and show the blocker; never issue a remote refund or silently destroy a load.

Assignment and custody are distinct. Stop, Move, Gather, Build, Craft, Transfer
or assignment elsewhere revoke future recovery pickup authority when the new
command is valid. A carried reclaim marker/load remains attached to its actual
worker until delivery. Stop physically returns the load once and then idles;
Move keeps it with the worker, with the existing immediate loaded-Move semantics.
Preserve the current disclosed return-first behavior for other existing loaded
work-switch commands. Receipt may settle the claim but never recaptures that
worker for the abandoned assignment. Empty departing actors release any unpicked
private reservation. Repeated same-job confirmation does not restart or duplicate
the job or its allocations. A paused camp performs no physical transition.

When unreleased reaches zero, empty assigned workers release future-assignment
references rather than making another source trip. Loaded workers may finish
their authorized return. The last receipt clears its marker/membership before
finalization checks. Remove the footprint/job only when unreleased and tagged
cargo are zero and no live reference remains. A manually held final load keeps
the job unsettled intentionally; cleanup cannot force its delivery or recreate
the outpost. The finite job can be explicitly resumed with eligible workers.
