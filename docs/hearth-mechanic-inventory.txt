Hearth depth on the accepted RTS foundation — source inventory, 2026-10-03

Authority and boundaries
The user accepted the new RTS core as a direction, explicitly kept no combat,
and asked for the old Hearth's complexity with the new controls/economy. This
inventory maps source behavior, not a claim that the old implementation fulfilled
all intentions. Broader sequencing is provisional until the independently reviewed
campaign specification is reconciled. Preserve both old Hearth and RTS saves.

Old source -> mechanic -> integration direction
src/hearth/engine.js TECH.knapping, learn(), practice(), workPerson(): discovery
plus practice improves tool work. First increment: a real workshop knapping job,
paid materials, assigned workers, progress, interruption/resume and a measured
extraction improvement. No timing-bar interruption or free generated stone.

TECH.bindings/baskets/pottery, cap(), worker capacity: carrying and stock capacity.
Restore carrying/storage choices after workshop crafting; upgrades must visibly
change a bottleneck while resource totals remain conserved. The old UI's capped
stock was not evidence of a finite map economy.

TECH.drying, preserved(), provisions(), BUILD.rack: a completed local rack reduces
camp food use and trip food costs. RTS has neither ongoing food consumption nor
provisioned travel yet. Do not bolt on a misleading preservation icon now; add
preservation together with actual expedition reserves/consumption and safe return.

practice(hunt/knap/gather), skills, knowledge: optional practice and ordinary work
teach skills. Old activities all shared a timing bar and hunt used generic food
patches. Restore substantive skill effects and readable learning; distinct
activity designs are pending the reviewed specification, not ported wholesale.

travelQuote(), migrate(), reveal(), ready(), settlement: persistent hexes,
visibility, residents, camp support and migration. RTS currently has one continuous
map, scouting and drop-offs only. Extend to persistent regional camps with exact
population/cargo transfer, finite destination resources and nonlethal recovery;
do not pretend an outpost alone already restores the regional system.

TECH.routes/quarry, dispatch(), tickRoutes(), recall(): provisioned neighboring
parties withdraw actual destination resources; safely return on shortages.
Next logistics increment should expose destination stock, provisions and physical
hauling. Respect finite deposits and avoid teleportation/double-credit on reload.

BUILD.fence/wall/gate/pen, enclosure(), herd work, TECH.husbandry/traction:
functional enclosures, animals settling, training a draft team, safe fence repair.
Integrate only with preserved RTS access/command rules, clear spatial feedback
and no animal-loss punishment. This is later than tools/storage/logistics.

TECH.sledges, road(), BUILD.road, travelQuote(): hauling capacity, crossing cost
and route improvements. The old implementation had sledges, yokes and abstract
pass roads, NOT explicit wheel/cart construction. Wheel/cart is a requested
extension to inventory and reconcile with the new specification, not an existing
implemented feature being restored. Physical throughput effects must be shown.

cultivation, BUILD.field/plow, workPerson(plow), prestige(): cultivation then an
actual field-work assignment completes the age; optional prestige archives the
world and carries capped traditions. Keep this substantive endpoint, not the
current RTS outpost/workshop preview gate. Cultivation renewal must be an explicit
input/work/land cycle rather than silently restoring wild deposits. Whole-age
prestige/handoff waits for the reviewed save and campaign contracts.

Current RTS strengths retained
Finite nonregenerating wild deposits; selection/Shift/drag/context commands;
visible cargo and delivery; responsive stable HUD; paid worker construction and
cancellation; pathfinding/ford scouting; local outposts; shortage/idle feedback;
independent versioned saves; no combat or offline resource loss.

First increment acceptance
Workshop is useful, not a decorative endpoint. A knapping project reserves 4
wood and 8 stone once, needs 12 worker-seconds at its actual workshop, can be
interrupted/reassigned/resumed without repayment, and finishes a shared toolset.
Tools reduce extraction time per item from 0.60 to 0.45 seconds; deposits still
lose exactly one unit per item and loads stay capped at five. Values are bounded
prototype tuning, not a final all-age balance decision. Existing RTS v1 saves
migrate without losing world/cargo/orders; retain the original pre-migration raw
save separately. Reject unknown future versions. Spark must test payment,
interruption, repeated actions, conservation, migration and the actual browser
crafting flow following earned expansion. No broad campaign additions in this
increment.
