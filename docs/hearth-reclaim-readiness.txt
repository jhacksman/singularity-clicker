Outpost recovery candidate — 2026-10-04

User-visible purpose
An optional completed outpost no longer has to lock its 60 wood and 20 stone
away permanently. Selected empty-handed villagers recover its structural
materials in real five-item loads. Only delivery to an operating store makes
those materials spendable. The finite world, existing walking/extraction rates,
paid tools, required eastern outpost, workshop and directly authored art remain
the basis of this opening preview.

Status
Source candidate only. No application, parser, test, build or browser execution
occurred on M6. The accepted b365ae01e4315bfd183a14438175c19c6396b11d preview stays
unchanged. This branch is not a tested replacement or a production release.
The copied contract is the frozen pre-implementation authority, not runtime
evidence. Independent source review does not establish a playable milestone.

Implemented boundaries
* Schema5 and the exact accepted-opening.outpost-reclaim.v1 profile. Canonical
  v1-v4 worlds can adopt claims without recorded payment history when both
  finite-material equations hold. Missing origin is explicitly unrecorded.
  A playable source that fails optional certification keeps the v4 path.
* Empty, settled optional outposts only. Recovery ends store service immediately
  but keeps the footprint through all unreleased or carried capital. Neither
  the hearth/workshop nor the last usable eastern outpost may be recovered.
* Pickup and receipt are separate tick transitions; no demolition timer,
  instantaneous stock grant, new support cost, deposit refill or labor refund.
  Manual orders revoke future pickup authority while carried custody survives.
* Finished accounting compacts into bounded summaries. Current refund origins
  and active cargo remain live obligations. IDs and counters retain their
  existing numeric bounds, with admission and reload headroom checks.
* Startup/import preserves sources before the durable write and active adoption.
  Incoming raw, outgoing verified live state, prior durable text and previously
  offered rejected recovery text remain distinct when their bytes differ.
  Navigation discovers all four new backup families without issuing claims.

Visible controls
Select villagers and inspect a completed outpost. #outpost-reclaim is labeled
Recover this outpost, or Resume recovery with selected villagers after retirement.
#reclaim-status states readiness/blockers. #reclaim-capital shows unreleased,
carried and received amounts at a retiring site. Economy text lists Recovered
separately from Gathered and Moved between stores. Stop and Return, immediate
loaded Move, other disclosed return-first work switches, pause, export and
existing touch/keyboard commands retain their distinct meanings.
