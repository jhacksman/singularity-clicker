Adopted requirements
Preserve the accepted opening and its finite deposits, no combat, autonomous
hauling, responsive controls, paid construction and safe return. Storage should
identify where goods actually are and what is available rather than showing one
ambiguous number. A transfer cannot credit a receiver before arrival or create
newly gathered output. Show real reserved and carried goods. Existing population,
stores and cargo survive save migration without a new grant.

Bounded implementation decision
Existing completed hearth/outpost drop-offs become named local stores. Add a
finite transfer order using existing workers and paths, with source/receiver
custody and real walking. Surface stock, availability, reservations and transit
separately. The accepted opening remains support-free and uncapped; no retroactive
loss or arbitrary cap is imposed on old saves. Existing instant reservation of
construction/knapping materials is a declared legacy abstraction unless explicitly
replaced and independently verified; do not claim every ingredient is physically
hauled merely because transfers are.

Dependencies deliberately isolated
V3 drying: 4 food +20 worker-seconds ->4 distinct provisions at a12wood/4fiber
rack; preserved goods use0.75 carried slot each. It is not an increased food yield,
less camp food use, unlimited regeneration, spoilage removal or recruitment-food
alias. Current core lacks fiber/water, explicit H2 support activation, provisioned
expedition jobs and a typed receiver. A rack now would produce an unusable object
or require invented consumption. Therefore no drying icon or unsupported benefit
ships in this increment. Next preservation work needs those connected consumers,
funding/return rules and capacity reservation first.

V3 caps100/item and200total and full-age support are prospective H2 content.
The generic overflow+200 rule and Hearth's cache+50 recipe need explicit local
reconciliation before adoption. Importing caps into the opening could strand old
stock or silently change the accepted fixture. Capacity activation/migration and
its disclosure remain separate from this local-custody step.

Tools018's shared0.45-second toolset is accepted prototype content. V3 ordinary
0.48 and sharp0.42 per-worker tool recipes/fiber/mastery are a different contract.
Retain and label the legacy toolset; do not silently reinterpret it or double-grant
new equipment. Restoring all craft/practice remains staged, not implied complete.

Acceptance
Source/deposit/store/reserved/cargo/consumption conservation; no transfer-output
credit; repeated assignment/Stop/reload retain ownership; invalid or unreachable
quotes have no debit; old saves keep their exact holdings. Browser play earns its
stores and supplies through ordinary controls, then moves a paid finite lot and
checks receiver credit only on arrival, user-visible reservations/cargo and exact
paused restore. Include meaningful source-local shortage/recovery feedback and
screenshots. Spark alone executes tests/builds/browser automation. No production
publication or whole-Hearth completion claim.
