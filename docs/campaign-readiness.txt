Cave and Hearth are playable. A bounded Agriculture first-surplus slice is

Authority
Existing saves identify Cave as 0 and Hearth as 1; preserve those identities.
The September 7 campaign direction was recovered through the parent thread;
stage-1-design.md and platforms.md preserve the September 9 Hearth requirements.
The accepted structure includes Industrialization at stage 5 and three ages
before singularity about avoiding extinction. Later names remain working names,
not independently approved complete specifications. Proposed acceptance slices
below do not settle balance, exact exit thresholds, resources or prestige rules.

Established direction: browser first; directly authored art; different gameplay
and perspectives across ages; increasingly immersive later presentation; upgrades,
automation and prestige in every age; earlier layers keep contributing and later
technology automates them. macOS/iOS are future required platforms, not shipped
packages. A final return to a simulated Cave remains a proposal.

Hearth's six accepted defaults: automatic time with pause/single-step; optional
post-completion prestige preserving completed worlds; fertile discovery helpful
but not mandatory; newcomers attracted to comfortable camps; safe suspension and
migration during shortages; authored first geography. No death/starvation/combat;
autonomous routine work; repeated orders must not accelerate production. Roughly
two hours of meaningful first play is a human experience target, not a timer to
pad and not established by CI.

Priority 3 — all-stage acceptance slices, in sequence
Later rows are proposals for concrete review, not approved mechanics or shipped
content. Each slice must meet the common gate below, not add a placeholder page.

0 Survival / Cave — implemented and regression-tested.
  Preserve authored clicker/events/completion. Verify repeated actions and refresh
  cannot strand the fire encounter; separately define any campaign bonus.
1 Fire and Craft / Hearth — playable; gaps above remain.
  Ordinary assignment through enclosure, trained team and actual plowing, plus
  established experience, recovery and preserved-world requirements in items 2–7.
2 Agriculture and Settlement — first-surplus slice only.
  Coherent settlement production and copper/exchange endpoint, with prestige and
  a defined Bronze handoff, rather than only today's five-harvest objective.
3 Bronze and Cities — absent.
  Proposed slice: copper/tin, furnaces/workshops and written accounting. Gate:
  repeatable bronze chain with a visible bottleneck, administrative benefit and
  normal recovery from a missing input.
4 Iron, Mechanisms and Science — absent.
  Proposed slice: regional infrastructure/trade, mills/gears, printing/scientific
  method. Gate: improve a connected network through an observable mechanism or
  research decision, not a renamed bronze recipe list.
5 Industrialization — absent; required position in the sequence.
  Proposed slice: first Factorio-like production/logistics, steam/electricity and
  machine tools. Gate: construct and automate a material/energy chain, diagnose a
  blockage and restart without resetting.
6 Electronics and First Computers — absent.
  Proposed slice: Cave-echo compute clicker, logic/transistors/information. Gate:
  turn manual computation into an automated machine with bounded resource costs
  and a visible benefit to earlier production.
7 Microprocessors and Automation — absent.
  Proposed slice: circuits/programming/robotics/factory control. Gate: configure
  an inspectable routine that handles real factory work, can be stopped/repaired
  and preserves its program and state through reload.
8 Networked Civilization — absent.
  Proposed slice: distributed logistics/internet/cloud. Gate: connect productive
  sites, expose capacity/reliability tradeoffs and recover a disconnected site
  without duplicating goods or compute.
9 Machine Intelligence — absent.
  Proposed slice: AI research/robotics/automated labs. Gate: a visible research-to-
  deployment loop improves autonomous work, shows cost/limits and recovers when
  delegated work cannot proceed.
10 Planetary Resilience — absent; first extinction-avoidance age.
  Proposed slice: energy/food/water/health/ecology/recovery. Gate: visible system
  dependencies and a successful resilience intervention in a reversible stress
  scenario. Exact risks/metrics require design.
11 Cooperative Intelligence — absent; second extinction-avoidance age.
  Proposed slice: authority/coordination/accountability/failure investigation.
  Gate: set delegation boundaries, inspect a coordination failure and restore
  correct operation; more agents alone is not a completion metric.
12 Distributed Civilization — absent; third extinction-avoidance age.
  Proposed slice: habitats/life support/repair/planetary defense. Gate: maintain
  distributed settlements through a bounded disruption with visible redundancy
  and repair choices. Exact hazards/failure semantics remain open.
13 Singularity / Cosmic Expansion — absent.
  Proposed slice: autonomous discovery, cosmic energy/compute/prosperity. Gate:
  coordinate expanding systems with legible constraints/outcomes. Review an
  ending and prestige semantics before any simulated-Cave return; preserve old
  worlds and player agency.

Common completion gate for every age
Distinct playable interaction; deterministic resource arithmetic; meaningful
upgrades; automation; explicit optional prestige/preservation; defined handoff;
legible shortages and normal recovery; authored art; accessible browser/touch
controls. Spark CI covers prerequisites, conservation, repeated actions, ordinary
full progression, refresh/import/export, supported migration fixtures and browser
completion. Record exact commit/commands/results. Humans assess pacing/clarity;
CI timing cannot substitute. No broad refactor is needed to document this backlog.

Platform gate
Physical-device touch, keyboard/accessibility, safe areas, foreground/background
and file/share behavior need validation. Native macOS/iOS projects, bridges,
packaging/signing and Apple verification are absent. Spark-only execution does
not authorize Apple builds on M6; coordinate an approved future testing approach.
