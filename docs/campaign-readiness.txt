Campaign readiness and next milestones — 2026-10-03

Numbering
The shipped code labels The Cave 0 and The Hearth 1. The user's references to a
working first stage and broken second stage may be ordinal. Use stage names in
repair discussions; do not rename or shift saved stage identities on that basis.

Recovered direction, relayed by parent from September 7 discussion
A fourteen-stage journey was accepted. Industrialization must be Stage 5, with
three pre-singularity stages concerned with avoiding extinction. The names below
were assistant proposals within that structure, not independently approved full
mechanical specifications. Recovered gameplay directions include farm management
for Agriculture; workshops and accounting for Bronze; infrastructure/mechanisms
for Iron; Factorio-like production/logistics from Industrialization; a compute
clicker echoing the Cave for early computers; automation, networks and AI in the
following ages; then resilience, accountable coordination and distributed life
support before cosmic-scale compute/prosperity. Later ages become increasingly
3D/immersive. Each age should have upgrades, automation and prestige; earlier
layers continue contributing and later technology automates them. The final
return to a simulated Cave was proposed, not a settled reset contract.

The later Cave/Hearth documents refine stages 0/1. Agriculture begins as small
cultivation in The Hearth; the next age makes agriculture central and larger in
scale. Do not claim the plow precedes all cultivation. Browser-first and directly
authored art remain requirements. macOS/iOS reuse is an architectural goal, not
an implemented native release.

Evidence-based repair priorities
1. Preserve the Cave and valid existing saves. Fix concrete arithmetic/lifecycle
   failures without changing its resource or prestige rules.
2. Make the Hearth's actual loop legible: maintain supplies, build, learn, travel,
   enclose animals, train a draft team, and plow. Show real blocked-work reasons
   and recover safely from shortages. Do not tune the economy based on an
   unverified assumption that learning or enclosure placement is impossible.
3. Regress full progression through public commands with periodic save reloads.
   Cover real browser setup, work, construction, learning, travel and persistence.
   A scripted completion time is not evidence of human pacing or enjoyment.
4. Before implementing Agriculture, recover its detailed discussed contract:
   meaningful decisions, feedback, automation, endpoint and transition payload.
   Preserve the completed Hearth world before any stage handoff. The existing
   separate v1 saves must remain importable; reject unsupported versions instead
   of silently converting them to a fresh game.
5. Deliver one playable age at a time, each with resource conservation, unlock,
   repeated-action, restart/import, safe-recovery and browser progression checks.
   Do not replace the unimplemented campaign with fourteen placeholder panels.

Unsettled by current repository evidence
Later-stage mechanics and milestones, cross-stage inheritance, optional replay
and prestige boundaries, migration between campaign save versions, and the actual
pacing/enjoyment of each age need recovered requirements and playtesting. The
stage outline alone is insufficient to claim those decisions have been made.
