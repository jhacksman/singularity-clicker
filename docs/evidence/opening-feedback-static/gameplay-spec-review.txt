Product judgment
The coherent small milestone is: finish a usable workshop, discover and fund its
existing Tools project at the actual supplying store, then use the tool-equipped
workers and see what limits a real delivered load. This turns the existing
workshop from an apparent endpoint into a comprehensible investment with a
visible consequence. The current UI stops its three promoted goals at workshop;
Tools exist only in its contextual inspector. That is a concrete player-facing
gap, not an absent recipe that needs inventing.

Accept this as meaningful completion of the opening's decision/feedback loop,
provided it includes an actionable path and actual useful delivery evidence.
Adding a fourth checkbox or a theoretical percentage alone is merely polish.
This patch does not add a new economic system or restore full Hearth depth. Do
not call it H2 completed, full-age progression, or an Agriculture handoff. The
canonical H2 includes fiber, water, prospective support, individual equipment,
ordinary/basket/sharp tradeoffs and practice. Those are incompatible with a
silent reinterpretation of the paid global Tools018 profile. Existing 4 wood /
8 stone / 12 worker-seconds and 0.60 -> 0.45 extraction stay intact.

The smallest stronger form within the same nonoverlap scope is a visible
"Use the workshop, then improve a supply trip" sequence. It should surface the
actual project, identify its named supplying store and shortfall, and present a
completed gathering loop's breakdown so the player can choose between closer
drop-off, existing tool investment, or another gatherer. It must leave those
choices optional; no newly compulsory outpost/recruitment bill is warranted.
No basket, support meter, invented reward or campaign transition is needed.

Measurement contract needed in the final specification
1. Use measured simulation time for actual receipt-to-receipt full-load cycles.
   No path-length/3 projection: the .1 tick movement loop discards per-cell
   remainder, so nominal speed does not directly predict a long trip.
2. The start and end must be actual ordinary gathered-goods receipts for the
   same worker, finite source and receiving store, with a complete five-item
   load and unchanged Tools state throughout. Total stock delta is insufficient:
   other workers, construction, transfers and reclaim can change it concurrently.
3. Classify movement before phase. phase='extract' also persists during outbound
   movement. Stationary extraction ticks are distinct from walking and receipt
   handling. Sum displayed parts to the same measured full cycle; call residual
   time handling/other rather than inventing a narrower cause.
4. Reject interrupted, partial/depleted, blocked, transferred, reclaimed,
   construction-supply, Move/Stop/new-order, redirected receiver, tool-transition
   and changed-topology windows. A reissued identical Gather must not create a
   better sample or accelerate production. Pause/wall-clock waiting contributes
   zero simulated time. Selection/inspection alone must not erase a valid cycle.
5. Before/after rows are two observed trips, not proof that the entire difference
   was caused by tools. Equal worker/source/store/topology still permits different
   perimeter choices, peers and rounding. State the known extraction rule and
   preserve actual travel/handling difference. Do not claim a constant 33.3%
   delivery gain, guaranteed payback, globally optimal allocation, or scalable
   per-worker rate from one sample.
6. Ephemeral feedback must say it was observed this visit. Reload/import clear
   it and clearly invite another ordinary cycle. A persisted tools=true flag
   proves crafting completion, not a historical post-tool receipt. Avoid a
   sticky first-delivery completion claim without persistent evidence. This is
   compatible with retaining the current save schema and exact completion flag.
7. Source depletion changes walkable topology. Buildings/retirement/cancellation
   and new stores also change routes. Stale route samples may remain only as
   explicitly historical records; they cannot read as the current route quote.
8. Show the source remainder and actual receiver beside the sample. Do not
   count the same five delivered goods as a new reward. The feedback must not
   mutate economy, orders, RNG, cargo, save contents, clock or replay behavior.

Progression and funding contract needed in the final specification
1. Keep at most two promoted major goals and one actionable constraint. Completed
   opening steps can be compact history. Do not stack five compulsory-looking
   objectives. Tools is an available continuation, not a rewritten H1 exit.
2. A workshop-inspection shortcut is safe if it only selects the actual object
   for inspection, preserves worker selection/orders and spends nothing. If a
   project exists, point at its original workshop; a second workshop must not
   imply a second project or re-purchase. Crafting still needs explicit ordinary
   Reserve materials & assign or Assign selected villagers input.
3. The quote must use knappingFunding's current named store and free holdings,
   including its reservations and existing project state. Aggregate top-bar
   resources cannot imply affordability at that store. A pending already-paid
   project must show remaining labor, not a fresh 4W/8S shortfall.
4. If materials are missing, identify the exact store/resource/amount and use
   the already available gather/transfer flow. Inspection/help/Cancel cannot
   change a worker's order or spend. Recruitment remains 25 food at the Hearth,
   optional, and is not framed as automatically best for every route.
5. For a finished/imported Tools save, show the completed investment and the
   current optional gathering action immediately; do not demand crafting again.
   Old paid/prepaid work, order semantics and raw backup retention stay as-is.
6. Separate historical command notices from live selected-worker/job status.
   Expiring a transient order toast is reasonable; saving failure, blocked input,
   invalid target and unresolved funded shortage need durable visible recovery
   or status. A blanket timeout must not hide the only warning/action.

   mid-loop, reload/import, repeated Gather and a paused wait. None may fabricate

   controls remain reachable, text is not behind the fixed tray, modal/Space/
   placement semantics remain unchanged. Any changed semantic shortcut needs
   actual keyboard and touch input checks, not dataset-only proof.
F. Source and migration regression checks confirm unchanged save schema,
   completion predicate, construction/Tools/recruitment costs, custody, art and
   existing stage navigation. Tests/builds/browser flows belong on Spark only.

Human verdict remains separate
Automation can establish truthful feedback and a reachable sequence. It cannot
establish whether an uncoached player notices the new continuation, predicts
which investment helps, enjoys the timing, or wants the following stage. For
that claim, observe an ordinary player complete the opening without authored
instructions, ask them to explain the workshop payoff and their next allocation,
and record confusion, idle waiting and chosen route plus owner Accept/Revise.
No forced stopwatch target or enjoyment claim follows from virtual seconds.
