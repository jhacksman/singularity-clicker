Why the new reads are live

The helper at test line 7 clicks Journey, installs waitForEvent('download') before
clicking Export, awaits that specific download, saves it to the requested
artifact path, and reads those downloaded bytes. The two invocations use distinct
before/after names and await completion sequentially. There is no substitution
of localStorage.getItem for the comparison and no reading an old artifact before
the new download finishes.

Frozen view line 251 exports game.serialize() when rejected is null. This fresh
context has no rejected import/save; the observed ordinary water-rejection branch
also requires ready() to accept a nonblocked camp. A storage/recovery failure
would instead produce the blocked warning and fail the exact toast assertion.
Thus this test cannot silently count a rejected-raw export as a successful live
game observation under its stated setup.

Selection and progression limits

Journey's cancelInput clears mode, ghost, owned keys and pending pointer; it does
not clear selected IDs or issue Stop. At this checkpoint there is no armed mode
or pending pointer. Selection remains the four IDs previously chosen by Select
all. ui() only prunes selected IDs that no longer exist. The later ordinary
deposit inspection preserves that selection, and the original contextual Gather
sequence therefore remains available after Resume. Any real browser scrolling
caused by focusing visible controls is accommodated by the helper's fresh map
bounding-box calculation; camera remains unpanned in this sequence.

The added UI operations can change incidental wall-clock timing and the initial
amount of idle simulation time before the paused baseline. They cannot change
resource progression here: no unit has begun work, no load exists, and the
comparison interval itself is paused. No test assertion relies on an identical
overall playthrough duration. Exact equality proves the complete serialized
world for this checkpoint, not equality of transient selection/focus/feedback
state, which is intentionally changed by inspection and rejection.

This is candidly an IDLE, EMPTY-CARGO fixture. Existing orders are idle, cargo is
zero, and there are no paid construction or transfer obligations to preserve.
The added comment correctly states that scope. Do not use its green result to

not establish persistence success merely by exporting; its purpose is live
