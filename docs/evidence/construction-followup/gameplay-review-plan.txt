Evidence verdict rules

Returned Spark PASS can establish ordinary-input reachability, exact conservation,
custody, work and fixed-policy outcomes when the exported state/inputs/screens
match. It cannot establish uncoached understanding, fun, natural attention cost,
real-time pacing, physical-device acceptance, full H2/Hearth or full campaign.
Keep source findings, witnessed failures, missing coverage and human questions
separate. No art or gameplay acceptance is implied before the actual PNGs and
ordinary Journey records are reviewed. Parent owns production corrections and
all execution/publication decisions.
