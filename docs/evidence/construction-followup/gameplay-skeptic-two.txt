Engine transition
startSupply at engine lines 214..222 chooses a remaining source when one is
available. Otherwise it routes the empty worker to the site, sets phase to
construct, and stores either "Installing <kind>" or "Waiting for site supplies"
in u.reason, depending on whether the staged vector already equals the recipe.

receiveSupply at lines 230..233 adds that actor's cargo to staged, records its
origin, clears its cargo, and calls start only for that same actor. No other
worker's reason is refreshed when the last delivery completes the recipe.

At line462, an empty actor already in construct waits without restarting while
materials are incomplete and sources are empty. Once the recipe is complete,
the exact same branch begins adding dt to b.work. It does not change u.reason.
The final carrier received after the recipe filled and therefore got the new
Installing reason from its own startSupply call. Earlier waiters retain Waiting
while they actually contribute the same installation work.

Ordinary four-worker trace
An ordinary outpost supplies 60 wood and 20 stone in sixteen five-item loads.
The player can gather the missing stock into b0, place a reachable site, select
the four original villagers, and let their Build orders continue automatically.
No malformed import, synthetic inventory, or special command is required for
the relevant transition. Consider an ordinary final wave in which four actors
are carrying the final four stone loads: sources is empty, staged is 60W/0S,
and the four actors each carry 5S for that same active site.

 Receipting actor    Staged afterward    Its new phase/reason
 first              60W/5S              construct / Waiting for site supplies
 second             60W/10S             construct / Waiting for site supplies
 third              60W/15S             construct / Waiting for site supplies
 fourth             60W/20S             construct / Installing outpost

Different travel times are sufficient but not necessary. Even if all four
receipts happen in one simulation tick, actors are processed sequentially at
engine line439. Each receiveSupply branch continues at line451. The first three
actors observe an incomplete recipe when startSupply sets their reasons; the
fourth observes a complete one. Once those actors have finished their paths,
their later construct iterations advance paid installation while retaining the
different reason strings. The stale label persists until another start/order
transition or completion, rather than correcting on the next ordinary work tick.

This is a source-defined feasible final-wave trace, not a claim that a specific
unexecuted placement gives all sixteen pickup/arrival times above, or that every
four-worker placement ends in this exact four-carrier wave. The necessary
condition is only one empty assigned waiter while another actor brings the last
missing load. The finite automatic multiworker loop provides that condition
without changing the player's assignment. Two such final carriers also suffice.

Current UI exposure
view line256 returns u.reason directly whenever cargo is zero. unitDetail uses
the same fast path at line265. Consequently the stale state is exposed in:
 - worker-card small text and title at line299;

Normal ui refreshes cannot repair it: they read the stored reason rather than
derive whether that worker is now installing. The frame loop at line339 keeps
refreshing this same text while engine work advances.

The aggregate site description is already more accurate. Lines288..289 derive
Next from the current reserved/carried vectors and report "Install the delivered
materials" after delivery, alongside the increasing work counter and full onsite
quantity. Thus the player can see a direct contradiction: the site is installing
and its work increases, but some selected workers say Waiting for site supplies.

Impact and contract
This violates the contract's requirement that selected-worker status and waiting
information reflect actual state. It can suggest that the supply line still
needs attention after all materials arrived. However the reviewed branch still
performs work and completes at the original bill/work amount; no additional
input is required, the aggregate counters remain informative, and there is no
demonstrated stock/cargo loss or rejected save. Low is the supported severity
for this isolated presentation defect. It merits correction before claiming
accurate construction-state feedback.

Minimal correction options for the root owner
The narrow engine correction is to refresh the physical constructor's reason
to "Installing <kind>" in the funded construct branch immediately before it
adds work. This makes an actor's reason agree whenever it actually contributes
installation, without restarting routes or altering orders, custody, or work.

For immediate accuracy when the player pauses on the final-receipt boundary,
the same current-ledger interpretation can instead be shared by unitStatus and
unitDetail, or the receipt transition can refresh only the site's empty current
construct workers. Do not call start on every assigned worker merely to refresh
text: that also clears paths/work bookkeeping and may replan or disturb intent.
Do not relabel loaded, manually moving, returning, cancelling, or unrelated
workers. A view-derived fix should cover both the card/status and detail paths.

Focused verification after the root fix
Use ordinary gather/build commands and four workers. Observe an empty worker
with Build(site), construct, no next order, and Waiting while another carries

is staged, require increasing site work and an Installing status for that same

Include pause immediately after final receipt, then Resume; finish the original
24 worker-seconds and verify the exact bill and custody totals stay unchanged.
The regression must follow an earlier waiter, not only the final carrier, which
already receives the correct Installing label in bc12f3aa.

Verdict: CONFIRMED omission, Low predictability/clarity impact. Reviewed the
current working-tree view.js and shell.html as separately authorized. At the
time of this read the construction command help and preview did not contain

The complete precommit path is:
 * shell line4: outpost/workshop buttons show their wood/stone bills only.
 * view arm(), line105: placement feedback explains site preview/confirmation
   and reservation, without an installation work amount.
 * fundingText(), line213: funding pool/store, missing materials, actual source

 * commandUI(), lines219..223: placement location plus that funding text.
 * touch preview at line157 and placement-status at line226: location, material
   bill, readiness/error, and Confirm site; no work amount.
 * desktop pointerup commits directly at line158; touch Confirm commits at
   line169. Neither has a later precommit numeric installation disclosure.
Only after commitPlacement sets the new site as inspected do siteUI lines287
and289 expose the fixed worker-seconds alongside actual work.

"After delivery: 24 worker-seconds to install" for an outpost and the equivalent
32-worker-second workshop phrase accurately states fixed labor after physical
delivery. Use WORK[mode] in the existing placement quote to avoid another set of
hard-coded numbers. It is not an ETA: hauling, paths, worker availability and
parallel participation are deliberately not converted into elapsed seconds.
Use the delivery qualifier only for newly placed physical-v6 sites. A legacy
playable camp can say "Installation: <WORK> worker-seconds" without implying
another material trip for its prepaid construction semantics.

The common command-help quote reaches both desktop and touch before mutation;
putting it only in touch placement-status would leave the desktop path uncovered.
Repeating it in the touch confirmation text is reasonable but not essential to
the bounded correction if the common quote stays visible. Preserve actual named
source reservations and material cost; the added labor phrase supplements them.

Rule alignment: contract Authority fixes 24/32 worker-seconds and requires full
recipe delivery before physical installation; the view requirements describe
reservation/delivery and postplacement build work. The contract does not state
an explicit numeric precommit-disclosure requirement. Accordingly I confirm a
small clarity omission and a compatible bounded improvement, not a new engine
rule, pacing promise, or missing mandatory simulation mechanic.

Addendum: source review of new G01 unit regression

Reviewed only the authorized current tests/hearth-construction-status.test.cjs
and the current funded-construct line. No execution was performed.

The test setup is feasible by source analysis. Lines11..15 gather d0/d1/d2 with
public commands and await idle empty workers. Their finite amounts plus initial
stock are exactly 116W/70S. The peers are moved with ordinary commands onto
(10,15), (10,16), and (11,17). These are distinct clear edges, not footprint cells,
of the eventual outpost at (11,15). Their presence therefore does not invalidate
placement; they remain outside the source-to-site path's other usable edges.

The sole builder u3 keeps its original Build order through fifteen receipts and
pickup of the sixteenth load. At the line26 predicate, staged totals75, u3 has
site cargo, and line28 requires no unpicked sources. No Move/hold/return command
is issued to that last carrier. Assigning the three already-on-edge peers then

edge: freeTargets excludes other actors' destinations/positions, not itself.
Each peer therefore has construct, zero path, and the explicitly checked Waiting
reason before the last receipt. This avoids dependence on an unproven natural
four-carrier arrival ordering while preserving ordinary command/custody rules.

The final receipt by u3 runs after u0/u1/u2 in the sorted actor tick. The peers
still see incomplete staged material during that tick; on the following explicit
tick they see the full recipe and contribute work. In old bc12f3aa their reason
remains Waiting, so line40 is a targeted negative oracle for this exact bug.
With the new root reason assignment immediately before physical construct +dt,
those peers get Installing outpost. The test also checks valid state after every
tick and an exact constructor/serialization reload of the installation state.

No setup blocker or false held-cargo assumption found. One small strengthening
would make the postcondition stricter: require installing IDs to equal the three
waitingIds, rather than filtering and requiring only installing.length > 0.
All three have been explicitly positioned with zero paths, and the test controls
their commands, so silently losing two expected participants should not pass.
This is not required to catch the old stale-reason implementation: that version
leaves all three in construct with Waiting and already fails the exact reason
assertion. The test currently covers engine reason strings and reload; it does
not independently assert browser card, tooltip, or selected-site DOM text.
