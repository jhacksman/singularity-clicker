IC02 immediate Move: independent custody and accounting planning review
Source: /task-3/hearth-rts-ic02
Commit: 3b15d6081f5396fcc579140c09d86e532674e2dd
Date: 2026-10-04

Planning verdict: feasible, but not a one-branch change to command(). The
transition, validator, save compatibility, duplicate detection and incoming
display must agree. The hazards below are review inputs before implementation,
not findings against an implemented IC02 patch. No app edits or M6 runtime,
parser, tests, builds or browser execution. The frozen /hearth-rts was untouched.

The approved contract (COMMAND-CONTRACT.md:13,64) requires loaded Move to start
toward its destination on the next running tick, keep the load, and hold it idle
on arrival. Stop remains physical return/deposit then idle (:11). A destination
beside a store is still a Move destination, not implicit delivery permission.
The same IC02 case also specifies cache/delivery behavior for work-switch; a
Move-only increment must not claim that entire acceptance case is complete.

1. Invariants to preserve at every accepted command and saved tick

- For each resource r, physical total is stored[r] + carried[r] + deposits[r] +
  spent[r]. Reservations are subsets of stock, not additional matter. s.stock
  must remain the exact aggregate of storage.stores (engine.js:33,73).
- Each transfer j satisfies amount = remaining + delivered + recovered +
  cancelled + sum(cargo held by actors with transfer===j.id). remaining equals
  sum(pending[actor]); those pending allocations cannot exceed source stock
  (engine.js:39-47). A loaded transfer actor has pending[actor]===0 and no other
  job ownership. Moving never changes those held amounts or resource types.
- u.transfer is the provenance of the held load, independent of the current
  manual order. It is not a resumable Move destination or a pending pickup.
  Preserve it through Move, arrival, Save/Load, Stop and the eventual receipt.
- Transfer receipts increment transfer delivered/recovered counters, never new
  gathered output. Gather-origin cargo has no transfer marker and increments
  s.delivered only on its first physical receipt (engine.js:132-138).
- Move/arrival cannot debit or credit stores, spent, deposits, delivered or
  transferred counters. Releasing an unpicked reservation may increase source
  availability, but does not refund physical stock or previously spent inputs.
- Paid building/knapping progress and material reservations belong to the job;
  moving its worker away does not cancel/refund/reset that job.

2. Required transition cases

Gathered cargo, including return in progress:
  Preserve cargo/resource. Cancel any queued pickup allocation using the old
  order/next before replacing them. Install Move now, clear next and dropoff,
  replace the route, and reset only worker extraction progress. No home detour,
  receipt, production credit or old gather restart. Arrival holds the same load.

Loaded transfer cargo (u.transfer set, pending share zero):
  Preserve the marker, cargo, resource and owning job. Replace the movement
  instruction, not custody. Keep the job alive even after old transfer order is
  replaced; current pruneTransfers already retains jobs referenced by u.transfer
  (line 131). Do not add held cargo to cancelled or remaining. Those counters
  represent different portions of the original finite transfer.

Gathered cargo with a queued transfer (u.next=transfer, no u.transfer):
  Release exactly this actor's pending share, decrement remaining and increment
  cancelled once. Keep the gathered load and its original resource. Clear the
  queued transfer before arrival so it cannot start after the new Move. Other
  workers' pending/held shares remain unchanged. Prune only if no obligations or
  actor references remain. Existing releaseTransfer reads BOTH old order and
  old next (line 129); calling it after clearing those fields leaks reservation.

Empty actor approaching a transfer pickup:
  Cancel only its unpicked allocation, then Move. No pickup or remote credit.
  If the pickup already committed on the prior tick, use the loaded-transfer
  case instead. The synchronous command/tick boundary decides which case applies.

Arrival and Stop:
  Arrival becomes a stable held idle state, with no queued former job, no
  dropoff, and unchanged provenance. Continue ticking without autonomous travel
  or receipt. Stop from this held idle state must still call the safe home path,
  preserve load until receipt, then become empty idle. If no store is reachable,
  retain the load and explain the wait. Stop at the original destination credits
  j.delivered; receipt at another eligible store credits j.recovered. Neither is
  gathered production. Repeated Stop cannot refund or deliver more than once.

3. Concrete dangers in the present implementation

- command():173 deduplicates against u.next||u.order. A migrated v3 actor may
  already be returning with next=Move(X). Reissuing Move(X) must interrupt that
  old return now; treating the queued order as an active identical Move silently
  retains the prohibited detour. Active Move duplicates should preserve the
  exact path/progress and accounting, not restart it. Define same-destination
  behavior after arrival separately; do not globally deduplicate idle orders,
  since Stop on an idle actor holding cargo must start a return.
- stores():77 counts incoming solely from u.dropoff. Delete the stale dropoff
  when Move replaces return/transfer travel, even if Move targets a store edge.
  No store should advertise that diverted or held load as incoming. A failed
  subsequent return must not resurrect an obsolete incoming destination.
- start():153-154 can install walk without unloading, and tick():197 can idle a
  walker without unloading. These support the mechanic, but the arrival reason
  must say Held with cargo rather than Ready for orders, and next must be clear.
- tick():196 removes the last path segment and continues; walk with path=[]
  exists until the following tick. A loaded-Move validator must admit that
  reached-target boundary or arrival must become atomic in that movement tick.
  Also cover a Move to the actor's current tile. Rejecting every empty walk path
  would create another ordinary save/reload failure.
- activeTransfer():106 identifies a job as active while a diverted actor still
  owns its load. Repeating Send for the same job currently returns the existing
  ID without movement. It must not misleadingly claim a held carrier has resumed
  delivery. Explain held custody or keep explicit settlement as the next action;
  never delete the marker merely to make the carrier eligible for another job.
- The UI currently says every loaded order returns first (view.js:40,62-66).
  Move requires its own immediate-carry/arrival-hold explanation. Work-switch
  behavior remains a separate contract decision; do not silently promise cache
  behavior from this Move change.

4. Validator and old-reader boundary, including the prior F6 regression

Current v3 explicitly restricts transfer cargo to return/transfer/idle
(engine.js:47). tests/hearth-rts-storage.test.cjs:217-230 intentionally rejects
transfer cargo in extract/construct/craft/walk/pickup. It caught resource
conversion and invalid custody; that guard must not be removed wholesale.

If walk is reused, admit a narrow new-version transfer-walk shape: matching
existing job/resource/worker, pending zero, one job owner, actual Move order,
no stale next/dropoff, nonnegative/reset worker progress and coherent target/path.
Keep transfer cargo in extraction, construction, crafting or pickup invalid.
Exercise malformed Move order/path/destination combinations independently of
valid generated walking and arrival states. Keep the old v3 invalid-walk test;
add an explicitly valid new-version Move case instead of deleting F6 coverage.

Recommendation: make this changed save-state contract explicit as outer v4,
while retaining storage schema 1. The old v3 reader then rejects every new save
consistently and preserves its raw recovery route. Without a version change, an
in-flight transfer Move would be rejected by the old reader while a held idle
transfer may be accepted; this is state-dependent compatibility, not proven
backward compatibility. Do not label it compatible without dedicated evidence.

For a v4 migration, do not change the legacy-storage condition from version<3
to version<4: that would rebuild v3 local custody at the hearth and erase its
live transfer/reservation structure. Preserve v3 storage, units, cargo, queued
orders, paths and payments; only change the outer version at migration. A new
explicit Move can replace a legacy queued return-then-Move afterward. Review
all exact version===3 guards, constructor post-validation, raw migration/import
backups and residue-repair version handling deliberately. Retain exact v3 source
bytes before replacement/adoption and keep backup failures blocked. Unknown
future formats remain rejected. This is a proposal, not an authorized edit.

5. Admission and commit boundary

Prepare all actor targets/routes and cancellation shares without mutation.
Validate the entire selected formation before releasing any reservation or
replacing any order. A mixed group's invalid/unreachable target must preserve
the complete pre-command serialization, including every actor's pending share.
Then release only selected unpicked shares, install all Move states, and prune
finished jobs after ownership has been updated. Current same-order command()
preflights all changing actors (:173); the UI's separate formation-order loop
also preflights before its first mutation (view.js:60-64). Preserve that boundary
or move it into one engine admission/commit API; do not introduce a new per-actor
failure after earlier actors have already changed.

6. Spark acceptance evidence required after implementation

- Earn gathered cargo naturally; Move nearby/across the ford/mid-return, then
  assert unchanged goods/counters until explicit Stop and physical receipt.
- Loaded transfer Move: exact job equation and resource type before command,
  first movement tick, last path segment, held arrival, paused reload, Stop and
  receipt. Test source/original destination/another store as eventual receiver.
- Queue a stone transfer behind held gathered wood, then Move; only queued
  stone reservation releases and wood remains held. Other workers still finish.
- Uneven 5+1 group allocations: Move either worker before pickup, and one loaded
  worker while the other is still pending. Cancel only the actual selected share.
- Repeat active Move every tick and compare exact state to one-command control;
  include legacy next=Move(same destination), same-tile Move, and repeated Stop.
- Move to a store perimeter and wait: no unsolicited receipt and zero stale
  incoming. Reload while moving and held; no route restart or new job resumes.
- Reject a mixed formation with one unreachable actor/destination and compare
  exact full serialization, not just aggregate resources or returned error.
- Keep all F6 invalid transfer-work imports rejected, plus simultaneous held
  cargo/pending allocation rejection. Test v3 pending/held/queued migration
  unchanged and new-format rejection by the immutable old reader.
- Actual browser commands must show immediate carried movement, held arrival,
  truthful incoming/cargo/next-action text and physical Stop return. Engine-only
  assertions cannot close the approved IC02 input evidence requirement.
