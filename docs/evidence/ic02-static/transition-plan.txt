Transition table
1. Empty actor -> Move: existing path/formation behavior, no economic change.
2. Gathered loaded actor in extract/return/held -> Move: preflight target/path,
   then replace old order/next with Move, route from current physical position,
   clear stale dropoff, keep exact cargo/resource and production/store balances.
3. Loaded transfer carrier -> Move: as2, but retain u.transfer and original job,
   including j.amount, delivered/recovered/cancelled, resource and receiver identity.
   Do not cancel/reclassify picked-up cargo or count it as new gathered production.
4. Pending transfer without pickup -> Move: release only that actor's unpicked
   pending allocation once, keep those goods physically in its source store,
   retain peers' pending/carried obligations, then Move. Prune only terminal jobs.
5. Gathered cargo with queued future transfer -> Move: release its unpicked future
   allocation; keep gathered load provenance and move immediately. No source debit.
6. Move arrival: hold exact load in place indefinitely; no automatic receive,
   free stock, produced/transferred counter, repeated gather, or latent next order.
7. Stop from moving/held cargo: explicitly replace order with idle and clear queued
   Move before physical return. Retain transfer provenance until actual receipt.
   The old Move must not restart after return. Same store receipt accounting applies.
8. Gather/Build/Craft from moving/held load: existing declared return-first work
   reassignment remains; settle cargo physically before work. Future local-cache
   contract is not silently claimed. Unpicked transfer reservations release once.
9. Invalid/blocked/out-of-bounds/mixed-group target: exact state unchanged, including
   path, next order, cargo, dropoff, job shares and paid progress. No partial group
   admission; UI formation preflights all actor destinations before committing.

Repeat and legacy behavior
Deduplicate actual current Move intent, not a latent queued Move. An older save
returning with next=Move(same target) must respond to the newly issued immediate
Move instead of continuing home. Repeated in-flight same-target Move should keep
its path/cargo/work/obligations unchanged. Stop on a held idle actor with cargo
must still initiate return; blindly deduplicating idle would strand the load.
Do not silently rewrite legacy queued plans during migration; explicit new orders
take the new semantics, while old snapshots retain their prior saved obligations.

Rerouting and accounting
build() currently reroutes all loaded paths home. A manual carryMove must instead
replan to its named Move target first; blocked replanning safely holds the load
with a clear reason. Route changes must never create a hidden return or receipt.
Clearing dropoff on manual Move removes stale store incoming counts immediately.
Only a later actual return/transfer receipt can credit a store; total material and
transfer amount partition remain constant through moving, holding and reload.
Paid construction/knapping progress is stored at its job and remains untouched.
Unit fractional extraction work policy must be explicit; it is not inventory.

Resolved worker-work policy: preserve the exact nonnegative remainder on Move
acceptance, movement and arrival holding. A later ordinary work start retains
its existing reset behavior. This supersedes the custody proposal's reset sentence.
Paid job progress always stays with its existing project.

Resolved active-route admission: new v4 carryMove requires integer, unblocked,
in-bounds nodes; consecutive nodes are cardinal neighbors; the first is the rounded
current cell or its cardinal neighbor; the tail equals the accepted integer target.
The fractional same-rounded-cell connector remains valid. An empty route requires
exact physical arrival. These checks apply only to active carryMove; blocked replan
is idle/order Move/path[] holding and need not satisfy a now-unreachable target.
This preserves existing grid routing, not a general collision-geometry rewrite.
