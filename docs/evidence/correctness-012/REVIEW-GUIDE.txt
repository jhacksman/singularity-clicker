open a terminal in the extracted singularity-clicker-review folder and run:

Use this shared browser address for navigation and save handoff. A save from a
different browser/address needs export/import; it is not automatically shared.
Stop the server with Ctrl+C after review. There are no third-party dependencies.

Suggested 5–10 minute review
1. Pause while planning. Choose Grain for Field 1 and assign two workers. Choose
   Restorative crop for Field 2 and assign one. One water carrier is assigned at
   the start; the fifth worker is free for materials and construction.
2. Resume and watch complete cycles. Compare food yield, seed and soil. Change a
   next-crop order during growth: the current harvest must finish normally.
3. Gather wood/clay with the spare worker. After two harvests, reserve a granary
   project and assign that worker to Construction. Reassign after completion.
4. Build irrigation, keep five harvests and 40 grain. The milestone is a first
   dependable surplus; fermentation, textiles, exchange and copper are not built.
5. Pause and reload; progress should remain. Journey exports the active save.
   Empty supplies can recover through water and wild food/seed assignments.

Hearth handoff
Finish the first plow in the Hearth, then choose Continue to Agriculture from
Journey. Confirm the new settlement. The original Hearth save stays unchanged;
a copy is embedded in Agriculture and Journey can export the preserved Hearth.
If you already have a completed Hearth elsewhere, export it there and import it
into the bundle's Hearth first. Do not overwrite a valued save without a backup.
