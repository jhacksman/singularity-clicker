Attempts to disprove and resulting limits

1. The quantity/resource are present elsewhere. The screenshot itself disproves any claim that all cargo information is lost: the aggregate is readable, and the inspector below the fold carries the complete selected-unit detail. styles.css:17 also supplies bottom page padding, so the fixed tray is not evidence that the inspector can never be scrolled into view. The defect is the inability to read the selected carrier's own load at a glance in the visible card/command area.

2. In this exact one-loaded-worker fixture, the aggregate happens to identify the same two wood. That reduces immediate practical ambiguity. It does not repair the selected-card contract: view.js:268 derives the aggregate from all in-transit goods, not the selected unit. With several carriers it cannot generally attribute a resource quantity to Aru. No multiple-carrier screenshot was supplied or executed here; that consequence is source-based, not an additional observed scenario.

3. The full status exists in textContent, and unitDetail is also assigned to title and the inspector. This is not evidence that screen readers lose it, nor that a desktop hover cannot reveal it. A title attribute is not evidence of reliably visible at-a-glance information for touch users. This review makes no assistive-technology or physical-device result claim.

4. The screenshot uses the owner's controlled clock and emulated touch checkpoint, rather than proving fresh-play comprehension on a real phone. Nevertheless, the visible ellipsis corresponds directly to CSS independent of simulation timing. Frozen-clock canvas timing or the older invalid-target toast does not explain away the DOM card's narrow one-line truncation. Neither is promoted to a separate finding.

5. More scrolling, a wider viewport, or selecting the card and reading the lower inspector provides a recovery route. No new loss of cargo, inability to Stop/Move, or irreversible outcome is shown. Those alternatives keep severity bounded and prevent treating P3 as a blocker for the previously observed scoped Move mechanics pass.

Bounded correction and acceptance requirements

Keep this a selected-cargo presentation change. A selected card may wrap its phase and load into short readable lines, or the existing command area may show a clearly associated selected-unit name, phase, quantity, and resource. A general camp aggregate, tooltip alone, or unchanged hidden textContent would not address the finding. Do not require a new game mechanic, change custody, alter the frozen Move/native sources, or broaden this into a mobile redesign during triage.

Acceptance should inspect a rendered 390x844 viewport, not only a full-page image or innerText: a selected loaded villager's name, Holding/Moving or planned state, resource, and quantity must be visibly readable without horizontal truncation, with its existing command controls reachable. Include representative supported text scaling and at least a second carrier with a different load, so camp-wide totals cannot stand in for selected-unit information. Verify selection changes update the association, and that additional readable lines do not cover or displace controls into an unreachable region. Future physical-device evidence, if required for mobile closure, remains separate from emulated browser evidence.
