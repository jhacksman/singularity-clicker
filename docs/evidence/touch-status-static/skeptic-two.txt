Attempts to disprove P3

1. Perhaps the text itself omits the quantity, or the screenshot captured stale
   state before the holding update.
   The source contradicts that explanation. unitStatus at view.js:239-246 forms
   "Holding · " plus cargoLabel for idle loaded actors. ui at :273 assigns that
   string to the card's small element. The visible card has already reached its
   Holding label. The same screenshot shows Carried: 2 wood and the lower Aru
   inspector says Holding 2 wood. The amount exists; horizontal card presentation
   hides it. No claim about the canvas's next animation frame is needed.

2. Perhaps image cropping or scaling, rather than product CSS, removes the end.
   The selected card is visible within the selection panel, alongside all four
   cards. Its status visibly ends in an ellipsis. styles.css:1 explicitly sets
   .unit-card small to white-space:nowrap, overflow:hidden and text-overflow:ellipsis.
   The row uses flex:1 cards, a maximum width of140px, a narrow-screen minimum
   width of65px and six-pixel padding; the floated portrait also occupies space.
   There is no selected-card exception that reveals the full status/cargo.
   This is a direct CSS mechanism matching the returned pixels. The image alone
   was not used to invent precise clientWidth/scrollWidth or glyph measurements.

3. Perhaps the aggregate Carried line already makes the selected amount clear.
   It supplies useful mitigation in this particular two-wood fixture. But
   economy-flow at view.js:268 reports camp-wide economy.inTransit, independent
   of selected IDs. It cannot generally attribute loads to one selected actor
   when multiple actors carry different quantities or types. This PNG does not
   itself demonstrate such a multi-load case; that consequence is an inference
   from the aggregation source and needs an explicit fixture for acceptance.
   I do not claim the player cannot discover the current quantity anywhere.

4. Perhaps the inspector or title provides the missing information immediately.
   The inspector does show the full selected load in the full-page image, and
   ui assigns unitDetail to the card title. Those are real alternate disclosures.
   The browser fixture sets a 390 x 844 viewport at hearth-rts-move.cjs:51, but
   captures this image with fullPage:true at :122. The lower inspector in that
   tall image is not evidence that its text was initially visible in the 844px
   viewport. A title attribute also does not establish visible touch disclosure
   without hover. The narrow finding is the card's at-a-glance readability, not
   that scrolling, inspection or every accessibility path is impossible.

5. Perhaps the controlled clock explains the visible problem and invalidates
   a product finding.
   The test installs/pauses a Playwright clock at :57-59, reuses an earned seed
   for the touch case at :105-107 and advances ticks under control. That limits
   fresh-play/real-time evidence. It does not change the declared nowrap/ellipsis
   layout or supply a reason why this string would fit under ordinary elapsed
   time. The test's :120 innerText assertion only checks /Holding/i and cannot
   establish rendered quantity/resource visibility. The evidence supports text
   clipping regardless of the separate simulation-clock limitations.
