Hearth RTS reset — first playable core

Problem observed in the old rules
Every food/fiber patch regenerates every tick; wood regrows, the spring holds
one million units, farms produce recurring food and there is no sustained
production sink beyond staged unlocks. Workers often stop at storage capacity.
A completion test does not establish a meaningful economy or satisfying controls.
The complaint that resources are not meaningfully limited is supported by these
rules, even though individual stocks have numeric caps.

First replacement slice
One readable continuous top-down camp map. Drag to select, Shift to add,
right-click to move/gather/construct, left-click inspect; explicit command buttons
and touch alternatives. Immediate destination rings and command acknowledgments.
Workers walk routes, extract finite deposits, carry visible loads and return to
the nearest completed drop-off. Deposits never regenerate. Construction reserves
its listed cost once, takes worker time, and can be cancelled for an exact refund.
Workers stop with a specific reason at depletion or an unreachable destination.

A hearth begins with four workers and small stores. Wood and stone fund an
outpost; food funds another worker. Nearby deposits are deliberately small.
Scout east and place the outpost near a second concentration of deposits to
shorten hauling. Build a workshop from actual deliveries to finish this slice.
The objective is one satisfying expansion, not a renamed full-age endpoint.

No combat while the parent's question is pending. No copied SC2 assets or AI
images. New map, units and feedback use directly authored Canvas/vector shapes.
No hunger death, offline depletion, forced restart or timed punishment. Planned
sites can be cancelled; a bare worker can always scout/gather remaining supplies.

Preservation
New entrypoint hearth-rts.html and independent hearth-rts.v1 save key. Old Hearth
entrypoint, v1 save, archives and Agriculture handoff are untouched. This new
prototype does not silently reinterpret old saves or claim full Hearth parity.
The existing game remains accessible by an explicit Previous Hearth link.

Acceptance on Spark
Deterministic conservation across stock/deposits/cargo/construction; repeat
orders, refunds, pathfinding, save/restore. Fresh browser play must use clicks,
drag selection and right-clicks for gathering, hauling, building, depletion and
expansion. Save snapshots may be read for assertions, never injected to skip
progression. Capture video and screenshots; inspect readability/feedback rather
than treating assertion count as evidence of interaction quality.
