Player-facing change
Move carries a worker's existing load directly along the accepted route and holds
it on arrival. A paused order starts on Resume. Stop and Return physically settles
the load at reachable storage, then idles. Other work reassignment still returns
cargo first and says so. This implements only the Move portion of canonical IC02,
not its work-switch/cache behavior or general Hold/automation/capacity systems.
No art is replaced, no images are generated and no production publication occurs.

Custody and routing
A dedicated carryMove phase has no production, pickup or receipt operation. Cargo
amount/type, worker remainder and existing transfer identity survive acceptance,
movement, hold and reload. Only the selected actor's unpicked future reservation
is released, before its old order fields are cleared. Stale dropoff is removed.
Arrival holds indefinitely; only a later physical receipt credits one store.
Repeated active Move does not reset progress. A new Move overrides an old queued
return-then-Move plan, while migration alone preserves that saved plan. Stop from
held idle cargo is meaningful and cannot restart the replaced Move after receipt.
Construction replans manual cargo to its named target or holds it safely; canceling
an obstruction does not silently restart the held Move.

The new phase validates integer clear route nodes, cardinal adjacency, rounded
current-cell/first-node continuity and the exact accepted endpoint. The final node
is reached within the actual movement budget, not popped early by a tolerance.
The prior F6 ban on transfer cargo in ordinary work/walk/pickup phases remains.
These are scoped grid-route checks, not a general movement/collision rewrite.
